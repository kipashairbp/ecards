Make the store-portal bank list admin-editable and add a full bank-details export

- Bank picker list now comes from GET /store-billing/banks: the built-in
  list (usbanks.js, now including Metropolitan Bank) unless an admin has
  saved their own under the store_billing_banks setting. Editable from
  Settings > Store Billing > Bank List (one per line, reset to built-in);
  "Other / Not Listed" is always kept last since it unlocks the free-text
  bank name field.
- Store Billing gets a "Download Store Bank Details (Excel)" button: every
  store with its payment info in full (account + routing numbers), gated
  to super_admin/org_admin exactly like the per-store reveal and audited
  as export_payment_info. Stores without payment info are included with
  blank bank columns, sorted last.
